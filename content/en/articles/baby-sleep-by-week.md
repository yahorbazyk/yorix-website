# Baby Sleep by Week (Weeks 1–15)

**By the Yorix team**  
**Published:** [Month DD, YYYY] · **Updated:** [Month DD, YYYY]

*This article is for general information and is not medical advice. It is not a substitute for care from your baby's pediatrician or another qualified clinician. If you are worried about your baby's sleep, feeding, growth, or breathing, contact your pediatrician.*

This page is an **index**, not a sixteenth weekly overview and not a month schedule. Each week link is a snapshot of that week. Official sources do not publish a different 24-hour sleep total for every week. The pages use the correct **age band** (the National Sleep Foundation 14–17 hours at 0–3 months, as quoted by KidsHealth, and other published ranges that do not agree with it) and only name a sourced landmark when one actually applies that week.

Month views stay in the month guides. Do not use a week page as a substitute for [1 month old sleep schedule](/1-month-old-sleep-schedule), [2 month old sleep schedule](/2-month-old-sleep-schedule), [3 month old sleep schedule](/3-month-old-sleep-schedule), or [4 month old sleep schedule](/4-month-old-sleep-schedule).

- [1 week old baby sleep](/1-week-old-baby-sleep) — first nights at home, wake-to-feed
- [2 week old baby sleep](/2-week-old-baby-sleep) — pieces, feeds, “sleeping all day”
- [3 week old baby sleep](/3-week-old-baby-sleep) — more alert days, shorter naps
- [4 week old baby sleep](/4-week-old-baby-sleep) — week-4 snapshot; the month view is [1 month old sleep schedule](/1-month-old-sleep-schedule)
- [5 week old baby sleep](/5-week-old-baby-sleep) — when a slightly longer stretch slips
- [6 week old baby sleep](/6-week-old-baby-sleep) and [6 week sleep regression](/6-week-sleep-regression)
- [7 week old baby sleep](/7-week-old-baby-sleep) — almost two months, still no timetable
- [8 week old baby sleep](/8-week-old-baby-sleep) and [8 week old short naps](/8-week-old-short-naps) — month view: [2 month old sleep schedule](/2-month-old-sleep-schedule)
- [9 week old baby sleep](/9-week-old-baby-sleep) — after the body-clock landmark may start
- [10 week old baby sleep](/10-week-old-baby-sleep) — short naps, uneven nights
- [11 week old baby sleep](/11-week-old-baby-sleep) — on the way to 3 months
- [12 week old baby sleep](/12-week-old-baby-sleep) and [12 week old false starts](/12-week-old-false-starts) — month view: [3 month old sleep schedule](/3-month-old-sleep-schedule)
- [13 week old baby sleep](/13-week-old-baby-sleep) — just past 3 months, between official bands
- [14 week old baby sleep](/14-week-old-baby-sleep) — a long stretch that may not repeat
- [15 week old baby sleep](/15-week-old-baby-sleep) — last week snapshot; from week 16 use [4 month old sleep schedule](/4-month-old-sleep-schedule)

## After week 15

Week 16–24 searches belong on the 4-, 5-, and 6-month schedules (and the 4- or 6-month regression pages when that name is used). For later ages, use the month guides.

The first-weeks overview remains [newborn sleep schedule](/newborn-sleep-schedule).
