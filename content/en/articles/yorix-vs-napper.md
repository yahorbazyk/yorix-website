# Napper vs Yorix: Which Baby Sleep App Fits Your Family?

**By the Yorix team**  
**Published:** [Month DD, YYYY] · **Updated:** [Month DD, YYYY]

*This article is for general information and is not medical advice. It is not a substitute for care from your baby's pediatrician or another qualified clinician. If you are worried about your baby's sleep, feeding, growth, or breathing, contact your pediatrician.*

*Disclosure: this comparison is written by the team that makes Yorix, so read it with that in mind. We limited ourselves to what each company publishes: Napper facts come from napper.app, its App Store page, and its Google Play page; Yorix facts come from yorix-app.com and the Yorix App Store page. Napper is a popular nap-scheduling app made by Napper AB. According to its App Store page, it was started by a dad on parental leave whose daughter wasn't a great sleeper. It builds a daily nap and bedtime plan from what you log, plays sleep sounds, and includes a short sleep course. Yorix, our app, also predicts the next sleep from your baby's real day, but it runs only on Apple devices and stores the diary in your own iCloud. This page sets out what each app offers, what each costs, and where Napper is the better choice.

## Short answer

**Napper suits you** if you need **Android** (or a mix of Android and iPhone in the family), want an **Apple Watch** app, or like the idea of a guided **sleep course** plus a large **sound library** (30+ sounds). It has a long public track record: **4.9 stars from about 4,700 ratings** on the U.S. App Store with an **Editors' Choice** badge, and **4.7 stars from about 17,300 reviews and 1M+ downloads** on Google Play. Napper's website lists **Napper Unlimited at $69.99 a year**.

**Yorix suits you** if your family is all on **iPhone or iPad**, you want the plan to **re-plan straight after each logged nap**, you'd like a **coach that answers from your own diary**, and you prefer a diary kept **on your device and in your iCloud**. Logging sleep in Yorix costs nothing; **Premium is $59.99 a year**, $14.99 a month, or $5.99 a week with a 7-day trial.

## Free vs paid: what Napper's pricing page shows

Napper's pricing page compares **Free** and **Unlimited**. In the free column, two features are available: **daily sleep tracking** and a **sleep schedule based on age**. Five more are shown with a lock in the free column and unlocked in Unlimited: **personalized sleep analysis, educational sleep courses, custom baby sleep sounds, trends and statistics,** and **"track everything."** The page gives Unlimited as **$69.99 a year**, and the gift page sells a one-year Unlimited gift for the same $69.99 that doesn't auto-renew.

**What we couldn't confirm.** The U.S. App Store lists several in-app purchases with different names: "Unlimited: Baby-Sleep Expert +" ($14.99, $29.99, $69.99), "Unlimited: Night Slumber +" ($49.99, $119.99), "Napper Sleep" ($14.99 monthly, $89.99 yearly), and "Napper Complete" ($19.99 monthly, $129.99 yearly). The listing doesn't say which of these are currently offered to new users or what separates "Sleep" from "Complete," and we didn't find a free-trial length on the pages we checked. Check the price inside the app before you subscribe.

## Where Napper is stronger

- **Android and wearables.** Napper runs on Android, Apple Watch, and even Apple Vision. Yorix has no Android version, so Napper is the realistic option for a mixed-phone household.
- **Proven at scale.** Over a million Google Play downloads, thousands of ratings on both stores, and an Editors' Choice badge on the App Store. Yorix has only a handful of ratings so far.
- **A structured course.** Napper offers a sleep and attachment-parenting course that it says can improve your sleep situation in 14 days or less. Yorix doesn't offer a step-by-step sleep course.
- **A bigger sound library.** More than 30 sounds, made with a composer, versus a shorter list in Yorix.
- **A free age-based schedule.** Napper's free tier includes a schedule based on age; Yorix keeps its forecast in Premium.
- **Gifting.** A non-renewing one-year gift subscription is available on Napper's website.

## Where Yorix takes a different approach

- **Re-planning in real time.** When a nap is cut short, Yorix immediately moves the next windows and bedtime for the rest of that day.
- **Answers grounded in your log.** The coach sees your baby's age and latest entries (without names or contacts), so replies reflect the day you actually had.
- **Your diary, your iCloud.** Entries are stored on the phone and synced through your own iCloud rather than a Yorix account. There are no ads in the app.
- **No signal needed for forecasts.** Forecasts are calculated on the device, which helps on flights or in places with poor coverage.
- **iPad and Apple ecosystem extras.** iPad layout, Live Activities, Dynamic Island timers, and Siri logging.
- **Lower yearly Premium price** than the $69.99 Napper Unlimited price shown on Napper's website ($59.99 vs $69.99), though Napper's App Store list includes other price points.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the Napper app free?**  
Partly. Napper's pricing page shows daily sleep tracking and an age-based sleep schedule as free; personalized analysis, courses, custom sounds, trends, and full tracking require Unlimited.

**How much does Napper cost?**  
Napper's website lists Unlimited at $69.99 a year (checked 15 November 2026). The U.S. App Store also lists other plans, from $14.99 a month to $129.99 a year, so check the price shown in the app.

**Does Napper work on Android?**  
Yes. Napper is on Google Play with 1M+ downloads. Yorix is iPhone and iPad only.

**What's the difference between Napper and Yorix?**  
Napper covers more devices and adds a course and a big sound library. Yorix focuses on a forecast that re-plans after every nap, a diary-aware coach, and storage in your own iCloud.

**Can both parents use the same log?**  
Yes in both. Napper lets you share profiles with a partner or caregiver; Yorix invites a partner through iCloud.

**Is Napper or Yorix a medical device?**  
No. Both offer general guidance and don't replace your pediatrician.

## See if a re-planning forecast helps (Yorix)

**Yorix** is free to download, and logging sleep stays free. If you're on iPhone or iPad, try Premium to see whether a day plan that shifts after every nap works for your family. It is a support for tired parents, not a medical device and not a diagnosis.
