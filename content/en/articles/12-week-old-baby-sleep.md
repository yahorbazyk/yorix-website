# 12 Week Old Baby Sleep: Longer Nights for Some, Still Not a Timetable

**By the Yorix team**  
**Published:** [Month DD, YYYY] · **Updated:** [Month DD, YYYY]

*This article is for general information and is not medical advice. It is not a substitute for care from your baby's pediatrician or another qualified clinician. If you are worried about your baby's sleep, feeding, growth, or breathing, contact your pediatrician.*

This is the **week 12 snapshot**. It is not [3 month old sleep schedule](/3-month-old-sleep-schedule) (the month timetable — 12 weeks is three months) and not [3 month sleep regression](/sleep-regression/3-months) (the place for "is this the 4-month change starting early?"). If bedtime falls apart 30–45 minutes in, use [12 week old false starts](/12-week-old-false-starts).

## Short answer

You are still in the **0–3 month** sleep band: **14–17 hours** in 24 hours (NSF via KidsHealth), with the same wider published ranges as earlier week pages. The AASM's official duration recommendations **have not started yet**; they begin at 4 months. We will not give you a unique official "week 12 total."

What may be new is a **longer night stretch**. KidsHealth: at 1–3 months, "sleeping through the night" often means **about 5 or 6 hours**, not a 12-hour, no-wake night. Newcastle Hospitals NHS and Derbyshire NHS, already used on the 3-month page, describe around **5 hours** as a possible longest stretch by about 3 months for some babies. Others still wake much more often. Both can be normal in a baby who is feeding and growing.

Days can look slightly more predictable, and a slightly earlier bedtime starts to be a reasonable experiment. Formal sleep training is still usually early (Sleep Foundation: often 4–6 months). If sleep has suddenly gone backwards after a better spell, read the 3-month regression page before you name a new event.

## What is usually going on this week

The body clock has had a few weeks to practise (Sleep Foundation's 8–9 week landmark is behind you). Morning light and a boring night are more worth protecting. Three-month well visits and, in many places, **immunizations** also land around now — extra fuss or sleepiness can follow; do not over-bundle (see [sick baby sleep](/sick-baby-sleep)).

Rolling may be starting for a few babies and is still in the future for many. If they are trying to roll, stop swaddling with arms in — [how to swaddle](/how-to-swaddle-a-baby).

## What parents often struggle with at 12 weeks

From recurring community threads, in our words:

- Wanting a "real schedule" because a friend already has a 7 p.m. bedtime. Some 12-week-olds can handle an earlier night; some still do better a bit later. There is no official AAP clock.
- A first **false start**: down after the routine, up 30–45 minutes later. That is this week's specialist page, not a reason to jump into a full training method.
- One 5-hour stretch that does not repeat the next night, and a fear that you "broke" it by feeding.
- Short naps that have not lengthened just because the calendar said 3 months.

## A slightly earlier evening (example only)

This is not the 7:00 a.m. / 7:30 p.m. grid on the 3-month page.

Try a *sketch* in which the last nap is done early enough that the baby is clearly tired, but not screaming, around 8:00 p.m. A short bath or wash, a feed, a song, down in the crib or bassinet **awake or nearly so**. If they are up at 8:35, treat it as a [false start](/12-week-old-false-starts), not as morning. Night feeds still happen. If an 8:00 lights-out produces a worse evening, you have learned something; you have not failed week 12.

## When to talk to your pediatrician

Ask if the 24-hour total looks far outside the band and days are a struggle, if you are considering dropping night feeds, if snoring or pauses worry you, or if a sudden change came with illness. Fever under 12 weeks (or 3 months, follow your local rule) is always a call.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is a good bedtime for a 12 week old?**  
There is no official time. Count backward from a morning that works for your house, include naps, and stay inside a realistic 14–17 hour day. Earlier is a trial, not a law.

**How long should a 12 week old nap?**  
There is no official nap length. Many naps are still one cycle. See [short naps](/short-naps) for later months, and do not force that plan this week.

**Is this the 12 week sleep regression?**  
Use [3 month sleep regression](/sleep-regression/3-months).
