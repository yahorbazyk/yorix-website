# 11 Month Old Sleep and Feeding Schedule: Two Naps, Three Meals

**By the Yorix team**  
**Published:** [Month DD, YYYY] · **Updated:** [Month DD, YYYY]

*This article is for general information and is not medical advice. It is not a substitute for care from your baby's pediatrician or another qualified clinician. If you are worried about your baby's sleep, feeding, growth, or breathing, contact your pediatrician.*

At 11 months, the day often looks almost like a toddler's: three meals, a couple of snacks, milk feeds, two naps, and a bedtime that's fairly predictable. Parents searching at this age usually want two things at once: a **sleep schedule** and a **feeding schedule** that fit together. This page covers both, plus what to do when your baby starts refusing one of the naps a few weeks before the first birthday.

## Short answer

Sleep needs haven't changed since six months: **12 to 16 hours a day**, most of it (9 to 12 hours) at night, plus **two naps** for most babies (KidsHealth). Clinical wake-window charts for the last quarter of the first year are wide, **3 to 6 hours** at Cleveland Clinic and up to **7.5 hours** at Sleep Foundation; the apps aim for about **3 to 4**. For food, HealthyChildren.org (AAP) says babies of 8–12 months need about **750–900 calories a day**, with roughly **400–500 of them (about 24 ounces) still from breast milk or formula**, spread over three meals and snacks. No cow's milk as a drink and no juice before 12 months.

| Item | 11-month figure | Reference |
| --- | --- | --- |

## How much sleep at 11 months?

KidsHealth gives 12–16 hours for 8–12 months, with a night stretch of 9–12 hours and naps from about 30 minutes to 2 hours. Newcastle Hospitals NHS says that by the age of 1 most of a baby's sleep happens at night, and that many babies keep waking at night through the first year.

**App examples (not clinical guidance):** Huckleberry puts the 11-month total near 13.5 hours. Napper keeps its 12–15 hour range and describes two daytime sleeps.

## 11 month old nap schedule

Most 11-month-olds still need **two naps**. Here the sources pull in different directions. Mayo Clinic writes that the morning nap often disappears between 10 and 12 months. KidsHealth and Boys Town Pediatrics both place the move to a single nap later, closer to a year and a half. That spread is why a few skipped morning naps shouldn't automatically mean "one nap now."

**If your baby starts refusing a nap:**

- Huckleberry (app example) says nap resistance at 11 months is often a sign the baby needs *longer wake windows*, not fewer naps, and suggests trying 3.5–3.75 hour windows.
- Try that for a week before changing to one nap *(practical suggestion)*.
- If you do move to one nap, Mayo Clinic suggests moving the afternoon nap and bedtime about half an hour earlier.

When the switch does come, the [15 month old sleep schedule](/15-month-old-sleep-schedule) walks through it step by step.

## 11 month wake windows

- Cleveland Clinic, 10 to 12 months: 3 to 6 hours.
- Sleep Foundation, same band: 3 to 7.5 hours.
- Huckleberry, 11 months (app example): 3 to 3.75 hours.
- Napper, 11 months (app example): 3 to 4 hours, sometimes a little more before bed.

These are guides, not rules. The clinical charts are wide on purpose, and wake windows have no official AAP or AASM standard.

## Feeding schedule for an 11 month old

HealthyChildren.org (AAP) publishes a sample menu for 8- to 12-month-olds. Laid out as a day, it looks like this. Amounts are the AAP's; the clock times are **our example only**.

| Time (example) | Meal | What the AAP sample menu suggests |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 7:00 a.m. | Breakfast | 2–4 oz cereal or 1 mashed or scrambled egg; 2–4 oz fruit; breast milk or 4–6 oz formula |
| 9:00 a.m. | Snack | Breast milk or 4–6 oz formula; 2–4 oz diced cheese or cooked vegetables |
| 11:30 a.m. | Lunch | 2–4 oz yogurt, cottage cheese, beans, or meat; 2–4 oz yellow or orange vegetables; breast milk or 4–6 oz formula |
| 3:30 p.m. | Snack | 1 whole-grain cracker or teething biscuit; 2–4 oz yogurt or soft fruit; 2–4 oz water |
| 5:30 p.m. | Dinner | 2–4 oz poultry, meat, or tofu; 2–4 oz green vegetables; 2–4 oz pasta or potato; 2–4 oz fruit; breast milk or 4–6 oz formula |
| Before bed | Milk | Breast milk or 6–8 oz formula, or water, then water or toothbrushing |

**Drinks.** HealthyChildren.org suggests 4–8 oz of water a day from about 6 months, no cow's milk as a drink before 12 months, and no juice before 12 months. Offer drinks in a cup; the AAP suggests finishing the move from bottles to cups between 12 and 18 months.

**Sleep-friendly feeding tips:**

- Keep the last milk feed **before** the bath or book, not as the last step of falling asleep. Nationwide Children's advises keeping bedtime feeds out of the bedtime routine after 6 months.
- Brush after that final milk. In the AAP's Brush, Book, Bed wording, nothing should touch the teeth after the toothbrush.
- **Never put your baby to bed with a bottle** (HealthyChildren.org).

## Bedtime and night at 11 months

- **Routine.** KidsHealth suggests a warm bath followed by stories or singing. The AAP's Brush, Book, Bed adds brushing and a book at a regular bedtime.
- **Drowsy but awake.** KidsHealth describes putting your baby down awake and, if they cry, waiting a few minutes before brief soothing.
- **Separation.** KidsHealth says separation anxiety often develops between 8 months and 1 year, so a short, confident goodnight helps.
- **Standing up.** If your baby pulls up in the crib and can't get down, see our [10 month old sleep schedule](/10-month-old-sleep-schedule).

## What can make an 11 month schedule harder

- Switching to one nap at the first sign of nap refusal.
- A milk feed as the very last step of falling asleep.
- Settling your baby with a bottle in bed; HealthyChildren.org connects it to cavities and ear infections.
- Juice, or too much milk, crowding out meals (HealthyChildren.org).

## When to talk to your pediatrician

Ask your doctor about feeding if your baby refuses most solids, gags or chokes often, isn't gaining weight, or has signs of a food allergy. For sleep, mention loud snoring, pauses in breathing, or night wakings that leave you unable to cope.

## Frequently asked questions

**How many naps does an 11 month old need?**  
Usually two. Most babies keep two naps until somewhere between 12 and 18 months.

**What is a good feeding schedule for an 11 month old?**  
Three meals and two snacks with breast milk or formula at most of them, about 24 ounces of milk a day in total (HealthyChildren.org sample menu).

**What are 11 month wake windows?**  
About 3–4 hours in app examples; Cleveland Clinic's 10–12 month range is 3–6 hours.

**Can my 11 month old drink cow's milk?**  
Not as a main drink yet. HealthyChildren.org advises waiting until 12 months.

**What time should an 11 month old go to bed?**  
Usually somewhere from 6:30 to 8 in the evening, roughly three and a half to four hours after the afternoon nap ends.
