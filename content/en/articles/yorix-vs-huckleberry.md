# Huckleberry vs Yorix: A Fair Comparison of Two Baby Sleep Apps

**By the Yorix team**  
**Published:** [Month DD, YYYY] · **Updated:** [Month DD, YYYY]

*This article is for general information and is not medical advice. It is not a substitute for care from your baby's pediatrician or another qualified clinician. If you are worried about your baby's sleep, feeding, growth, or breathing, contact your pediatrician.*

*Disclosure: Yorix makes this page, so we have an interest in the outcome. To keep it fair, every Huckleberry detail below comes from Huckleberry's own website and App Store listing, and every Yorix detail from ours, all checked on 15 November 2026. Prices are U.S. App Store and website prices on that date and can change. If something is out of date, please tell us and we will correct it.*

Huckleberry is one of the best-known baby sleep apps. It is widely used, highly rated, and backed by a pediatric team. Yorix is a newer, iPhone-only app focused on predicting the next sleep and re-planning the day as it goes. Both log sleep and feeds and both predict nap times. The real differences are in pricing structure, devices, languages, extras such as expert sleep plans, and where your data lives.

## Short answer

Choose **Huckleberry** if you want an established app with **Android and Apple Watch** support, a **free tier** for logging all core activities, **SweetSpot®** nap predictions (Plus or Premium), and, in Premium, **expert-designed sleep plans** and the **Berry** AI chat. Huckleberry's listed prices are **$11.99/month or $68.99/year for Plus** and **$14.99/month or $119.99/year for Premium**.

Choose **Yorix** if you use an **iPhone or iPad** and want a next-sleep forecast that **recalculates the day after every logged nap**, an **AI coach** that answers from your own diary, **20 interface languages**, and a diary stored **on your device and in your own iCloud**. Sleep logging in Yorix is free; Premium is **$5.99/week (7-day free trial), $14.99/month, or $59.99/year**.

## Where Huckleberry is stronger

- **Platforms.** Android, Apple Watch, and iPhone, which matters if your household mixes phones.
- **Track record and scale.** About 79,000 App Store ratings averaging 4.9, an Editors' Choice badge, and a company statement that it is trusted by over 5 million families.
- **Expert sleep plans.** Premium includes custom sleep plans with regular check-ins, designed by Huckleberry's experts. Huckleberry says its sleep guidance never requires "crying it out."
- **A generous free tier** for logging almost everything, including a contraction timer, a breastfeeding timer, and solids tracking.
- **Monthly flexibility** for Plus at $11.99.

## Where Yorix is different

- **The plan re-plans itself.** Log a short nap or a late wake-up and the rest of the day's windows are recalculated straight away.
- **Diary-aware AI coach.** Answers draw on your own entries, such as your baby's age and recent logs.
- **Languages.** 20 interface languages, useful for multilingual families and caregivers.
- **Data storage.** The diary lives on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. Yorix says it cannot read it, the AI coach receives only a short summary without names or contacts, and the app shows no ads.
- **Offline forecasts.** Forecasts are computed on the device, so they work without a signal.
- **Annual price.** $59.99 a year for Premium.

## Things to know before you choose

- **Yorix is newer.** It has far fewer ratings than Huckleberry, so there is less public feedback to go on.
- **Yorix is Apple-only.** If anyone in your family uses Android, Huckleberry is the more practical shared log.
- **Neither app replaces your pediatrician.** Both describe their guidance as informational.
- **Predictions are estimates.** Any nap prediction is a starting point. Your baby's cues come first. Our [wake windows by age](/wake-windows-by-age) guide explains the ranges that sleep apps build on.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the Huckleberry app free?**  
Huckleberry has a free tier with logging and reports. SweetSpot® predictions need Plus or Premium (Huckleberry pricing page).

**How much does Huckleberry cost?**  
Plus is $11.99/month or $68.99/year; Premium is $14.99/month or $119.99/year (U.S. prices on 15 November 2026).

**What is Huckleberry SweetSpot?**  
Huckleberry's feature that predicts nap and sleep times, available from 2 months on Plus and Premium.

**Is Yorix available on Android?**  
No. Yorix runs on iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later).

**Can two parents use the same log?**  
Yes, in both apps. Huckleberry caregivers sign in to the same account; Yorix partners join through an invite and see the same diary.

**Which app is better?**  
It depends on your devices, language, budget, and whether you want expert sleep plans (Huckleberry Premium) or a self-adjusting daily plan with on-device forecasts (Yorix).

## Try the next-nap forecast (Yorix)

**Yorix** is free to download, and sleep logging is free. Premium adds the next-sleep forecast, the self-adjusting day plan, and the AI coach. It is a support for tired parents, not a medical device and not a diagnosis.
