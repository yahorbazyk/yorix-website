```markdown
## When a new sibling arrives

A toddler’s sleep can wobble when a baby comes home: later bedtimes, more curtain calls, a sudden need to be in your room, or a nap that falls apart. That is a **change in the house**, not a new official “regression month.” Keep the same short routine and the same bed, if you can. Give the older child a little predictable time with you that is not only about the baby. Do not start a second, more complicated sleep-training program on the baby’s first night home unless you were already in the middle of a plan that is still appropriate.

If two children will share a room, use [siblings sharing a room](/siblings-sharing-a-room) (and wait until the infant is ready to leave the parental room). The everyday timetable for this age stays on [2 year old sleep schedule](/2-year-old-sleep-schedule). Night fears and “won’t stay in bed” have their own pages; do not diagnose a new disorder from a few hard weeks.
```

```markdown
## If you searched for a 19, 20, 21, 22, or 23 month sleep schedule

Those months sit between this 18-month page and [2 year old sleep schedule](/2-year-old-sleep-schedule). We will not publish a separate official timetable for each month. AASM recommends **11–14 hours** in 24 hours across the 1–2 year band; the next official band is 10–13 hours at 3–5 years, which does not start at 23 months.

- **19–20 months:** still the one-nap view on this page. Bedtime battles belong on [toddler bedtime battles](/toddler-bedtime-battles) and, if you are using that name, [sleep regression at 18 months](/sleep-regression/18-months).
- **21–22 months:** if the nap is shortening or some days have no nap, use [when do toddlers stop napping](/when-do-toddlers-stop-napping) — but many children still need the nap at this age. Do not drop it only because a search showed a 21-month schedule.
- **23 months:** you are on the doorstep of the 2-year page. Use [2 year old sleep schedule](/2-year-old-sleep-schedule) for nap questions, stalling, early waking, and crib-to-bed. Named "2 year regression" queries go to [sleep regression at 2 years](/sleep-regression/2-years).

Daycare nap rules can fight a home one-nap day; that problem lives on [daycare and baby sleep](/daycare-and-baby-sleep), not on a new 20-month URL.
```
