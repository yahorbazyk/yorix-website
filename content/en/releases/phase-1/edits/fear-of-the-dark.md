**Why won’t my baby or toddler sleep after we moved house?**
A new room, new noises, and a temporarily unpacked crib can unsettle sleep. That is a change of place, not a new official regression age. Keep the same safe-sleep rules in the new home: back, firm, flat, clear, in your room if they are still in the first months. Rebuild the same short bedtime routine you already use — see [bedtime routine for baby](/bedtime-routine-for-baby).
