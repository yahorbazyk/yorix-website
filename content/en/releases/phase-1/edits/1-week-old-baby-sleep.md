**Why won’t my baby sleep after a hospital stay?**
Hospital days are noisy, brightly lit, and full of checks. Nights at home often look bumpier for a while. That is not an official “hospital sleep regression.” Keep the same safe-sleep rules you use every night (back, firm, flat, clear). If they are still recovering from an illness, stay on this page for congestion, fever, and when to call. If the stay was in a NICU, use [preemie sleep](/preemie-sleep) and corrected age, not a new URL. The first night home after birth belongs on [1 week old baby sleep](/1-week-old-baby-sleep) and [newborn sleep schedule](/newborn-sleep-schedule).
