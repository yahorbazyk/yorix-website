## Early wakings at 6–12 weeks

A 5 a.m. or 6 a.m. start at 6–12 weeks is often still a day–night confusion or a late bedtime problem, not the later "early morning waking" pattern described for older babies. Use the contrast plan already on this page (brighter days, boring nights). The later, after-4-months plan lives on [early morning waking](/early-morning-waking) — do not paste that full plan here.
