```markdown
**Do babies need blackout curtains or a pitch-black room?**
There is no official AAP lux number, and no rule that every baby must sleep in pitch black. What official sources do support is a clear day–night difference: more light and ordinary household noise by day, a dimmer, boring room at night (see [newborn day-night confusion](/newborn-day-night-confusion)). A night light, if you use one, stays dim — that is the rest of this page. Blackout blinds or a dark room can be a practical experiment for [early morning waking](/early-morning-waking) when street light or an early sunrise is in play. They are not a medical device and not a reason to skip the wind-down.
```
