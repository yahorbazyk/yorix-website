**Where:** FAQ.

```markdown
**Should I start sleep training at 8 or 12 weeks?**
Usually not. Sleep Foundation generally discusses readiness around 4–6 months. A hard patch at 6–8 weeks is not a reason to start a formal method — see [6 week sleep regression](/6-week-sleep-regression) and [when to start sleep training](/when-to-start-sleep-training) already on this page's short answer.
```

```markdown
**What if “drowsy but awake” does not work?**
It is a popular phrase, not an official AAP protocol, and it does not work for every baby or every night. If they cry as soon as they hit the mattress, you are in [sleep associations](/sleep-associations) and [baby won't sleep in crib](/baby-wont-sleep-in-crib) territory. If you are considering a formal plan, see [when to start sleep training](/when-to-start-sleep-training) and [is sleep training harmful](/is-sleep-training-harmful) — those pages, not a new “drowsy but awake failed” URL.
```
