## 10. How to stop contact naps (later months)

**Targets:** `/contact-naps` and `/baby-wont-sleep-in-crib`

**Where:** FAQ. Do not create `/how-to-stop-contact-naps`.

```markdown
**How do I stop contact naps at 6 or 9 months?**
There is no official week when contact naps must end. If you want more crib naps, the practical home is [baby won't sleep in crib](/baby-wont-sleep-in-crib), not a new URL. Keep every sleep you do get onto a safe surface if you need to put them down; a sofa or an armchair is still not a safe sleep space. Later-month contact naps are a habit and a logistics question, not a new medical event.
```
