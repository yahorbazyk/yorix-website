**Why does my baby wake up at 2 a.m. or 3 a.m.?**
There is no official 2 a.m. or 3 a.m. event, and we will not add a new clock URL for each hour. A waking in the middle of the night is usually a night waking or a [split night](/split-nights) — a long stretch of being awake between two sleeps — not a separate diagnosis. If the clock is closer to morning, [why does my baby wake up at 4am](/why-does-my-baby-wake-up-at-4am) and [early morning waking](/early-morning-waking) are the closer pages. For getting through the night as a parent, see [survive night wakings](/survive-night-wakings-new-parents).
