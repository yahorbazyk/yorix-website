```markdown
## If you searched for a 13 month old sleep schedule

Thirteen months is just past the first birthday. There is no separate official 13-month timetable, and we will not publish one. AASM recommends **11–14 hours** in 24 hours at 1–2 years (the same band used on this page and on [15 month old sleep schedule](/15-month-old-sleep-schedule)).

Most 13-month-olds are still on two naps. Do not drop to one nap only because a search suggested a 13-month schedule. The 2-to-1 discussion lives on the 15-month page and on [the 2-to-1 nap transition](/2-to-1-nap-transition). If sleep wobbled around walking, standing in the crib, or the first birthday, use [sleep regression at 12 months](/sleep-regression/12-months).
```

**Where on `/15-month-old-sleep-schedule`:** FAQ.

```markdown
**I have a 13 month old. Should I use this page?**
Use this page if you are already asking about dropping to one nap. For the just-after-birthday view, start with [12 month old sleep schedule](/12-month-old-sleep-schedule). There is no separate 13-month URL.
```

**Where on `/sleep-regression/12-months`:** FAQ.

```markdown
There is no official 13-month event. Use this 12-month page for wobbles around the first birthday and walking, and [15 month old sleep schedule](/15-month-old-sleep-schedule) if the question is really the move toward one nap.
```

```markdown
## If you searched for a 16 month old sleep schedule

Sixteen months is a common moment to *be in* the 2-to-1 transition, not a reason to invent a new month URL. AASM still recommends **11–14 hours** in 24 hours at 1–2 years. This page is the transition plan; [15 month old sleep schedule](/15-month-old-sleep-schedule) is the month view that already discusses two-nap and one-nap days.

Parent communities often describe mixed days at 16 months (one nap some days, two on others) or a "16 month regression." Mixed days are expected during the switch — see "If it is not working" on this page. A regression-named search can start on [sleep regression](/sleep-regression); there is no official 16-month event, and we will not add a 16-month row as if there were one.
```

```markdown
**What about 16 months?**
Use this page and [the 2-to-1 nap transition](/2-to-1-nap-transition). There is no separate 16-month schedule URL. Keep the 11–14 hour band.
```
