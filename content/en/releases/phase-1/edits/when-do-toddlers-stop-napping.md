```markdown
**What is “quiet time” if they will not nap?**  
It is the same afternoon slot, used for rest rather than sleep: books, puzzles, or a calm room, not a new show or a late car nap (Sleep Foundation; KidsHealth; already in “How to make the change” above). Some days they will still fall asleep; that is all right. The preschool timetable, including quiet time on no-nap days, also lives on [4 year old sleep schedule](/4-year-old-sleep-schedule). ```markdown
```
```markdown
**My 18 month old or 2 year old won't nap. Is it time to drop it?**
Not automatically. Many children still need a nap at 18 months, and plenty still need one at two. Use the readiness signs already on this page, plus the month view: [18 month old sleep schedule](/18-month-old-sleep-schedule) or [2 year old sleep schedule](/2-year-old-sleep-schedule). If daycare has already dropped the nap and home days have fallen apart, use [daycare and baby sleep](/daycare-and-baby-sleep). Do not create `/18-month-old-wont-nap`.
```
