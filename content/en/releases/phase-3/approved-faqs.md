## 1. `/should-you-wake-a-sleeping-baby`

**Where:** Add to `## Frequently asked questions`.

```markdown
**How long can a 6 week old sleep at night without eating?**
There is no official 6-week limit. In the first weeks, KidsHealth says to wake about every 3–4 hours until weight gain is going well; HealthyChildren.org's formula Q&A says to wake a formula-fed baby in the first month if they sleep more than 4–5 hours. By 6 weeks, many babies with good weight gain are allowed a longer stretch — but only if your pediatrician has already said so. The 6-week snapshot is on [6 week old baby sleep](/6-week-old-baby-sleep).

**Can I let a newborn sleep through the night?**
Not as a default in the first days. Use the wake-to-feed rules already on this page, then ask your pediatrician when a longer stretch is all right. "Through the night" at 1–3 months often means about 5–6 hours (KidsHealth), not 12 hours.
```

**Can a baby nap in a swing, bouncer, or "only if I am watching"?**
A swing or bouncer is not an approved sleep space. If they doze there, move them to a firm, flat, on-the-back surface as soon as you can — the same rule as a car seat after you arrive. Supervised does not make an incline safe for sleep. See also [naps on the go](/naps-on-the-go).

**Where:** FAQ on `/bed-sharing-and-co-sleeping` (primary); a one-line pointer on `/safe-sleep-for-babies` if needed.

```markdown
**Can I let my newborn sleep on my chest?**
A hold while you are awake and watching is comfort, not a sleep space. If you fall asleep with a baby on your chest on a sofa or armchair, the risk is higher than in a clear crib. Put them on their back, in their own firm, flat space, when you need to sleep. This page already explains room-sharing without bed-sharing (AAP) and the UK overlap.
```

## 9. `/baby-snoring-and-mouth-breathing`

**Where:** FAQ.

```markdown
**My 2 week old sleeps with their mouth open. Is that normal?**
A briefly open mouth in active sleep can happen. If the baby is congested, having trouble feeding, or you hear regular snoring or pauses, use the advice already on this page and [sick baby sleep](/sick-baby-sleep). Newborns with persistent mouth breathing or poor feeding should be discussed with the pediatrician, not diagnosed from a photo.
```

### Phase 3 — Pilot FAQ additions: 10. `/crib-to-toddler-bed`

**Action:** paste

## 10. `/crib-to-toddler-bed`

**Where:** After the bunk-bed / crib-tent merge if that has been pasted, or in the FAQ.

```markdown
**Can a toddler sleep with a pillow, or on a mattress on the floor?**
Pillows stay out of infant cribs. For a toddler in a bed, follow the dentist- and pediatrician-advised, age-appropriate bedding already discussed with your clinician, and keep the space clear of hanging cords and top-heavy furniture. A mattress on the floor is a household setup, not an AAP "floor bed" protocol; if you use one, it still needs a clear, smoke-free, safe room (no adult pillows piled as bumpers). This is not a product review.
```
