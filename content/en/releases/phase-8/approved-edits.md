```markdown
**Does a bedtime massage help a baby sleep?**
Some families find a short, gentle massage calming, and NHS baby-massage leaflets treat it mainly as a bonding activity. A Cochrane review of massage for infants under 6 months found the evidence not strong enough to recommend it for mental or physical health in low-risk babies. It is not a sleep-training method and not a reason to add essential oils unless a clinician has told you to. Stop if the baby is unwell, has a fever or broken skin, or is clearly unhappy. Keep the rest of the routine boring and consistent.
```

### A. `/sick-baby-sleep` — ear infection nights

Add this FAQ (or a short bullet under “When to get help”). Do not add dosing.

```markdown
**Can an ear infection ruin nights, and should I prop them up?**
Ear pain often feels worse when a child lies down, so nights can be miserable during an infection. That is not a reason to tilt the mattress, add a pillow, or leave them in a car seat or swing to sleep. Keep them on their back, on a firm flat surface. Call the pediatrician if you suspect an ear infection (fever, pulling at the ear, a baby who is not themselves). Ask that clinician about pain relief — we will not name or dose medicines here. Congestion care stays in the section above; the humidifier itself is on [humidifiers and baby sleep](/humidifiers-and-baby-sleep).
```
